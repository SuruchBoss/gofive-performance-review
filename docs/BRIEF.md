# Product Owner Exam

## **Timeline:** Submit within 7 days of receiving the brief

---

## Before you start

At Gofive we believe in getting things done and owning work end to end. The PO we're looking for is someone who can carry an idea all the way from "a problem" to "something you can actually use" on their own — without waiting in line for the Dev team.

So this brief isn't about writing a PRD or a spec. We want to see you **vibe code / vibe design a working prototype.** Use AI as much as you like.

We care about **outcomes** above all: something a user can actually click through, and the decisions behind why you shipped it this way.

---

## Business context

empeo is an all-in-one people management platform (HRM + HRD) used by 1,000+ organizations, from small businesses to publicly listed companies.

One of its key domains is **Performance Management** — evaluating and developing employee performance. Plenty of organizations still run their review cycles on spreadsheets and email, and hit the same problems over and over: managers review late, employees can't see their own goals, HR can't consolidate the data, and every manager rates on a different scale.

**The challenge:** we'd love you to help **transform performance reviews into a modern system** that answers the big question every organization runs into — by designing and **building a working prototype** of a Performance Management feature on empeo:

> **How do we make annual performance reviews *fair* across an entire organization, and genuinely surface who the high performers and low performers are?**
> 

Reference (worth exploring for real):

- https://www.empeo.com

---

## Part 1 — Build it (vibe code / vibe design)

Performance Management spans a lot — from HR setting up a review cycle, to managers evaluating their teams, to employees seeing their own goals and results.

**What to submit:** a link to a prototype you can actually click through the main flow (built with any tool you like).

**What we'd love this prototype to deliver (the big-picture objectives):**

- **Fairness:** bring reviews onto a shared standard across the organization, reducing the inconsistency and bias between individual managers.
- **Reflecting real performance:** let the organization see, in a meaningful way, who the high and low performers are — not just a floating score.
- Let multiple roles (HR, managers, employees) work together in one system, where each person sees and does what's relevant to them.
- Account for the moments when things don't go to plan (e.g. someone misses the cycle deadline).

---

## Part 2 — Questions (a few sentences each is plenty)

1. **Scope & prioritization:** what's in your v1, and what did you **deliberately leave out** — and why? If you had to ship this to a real customer next week, is this the right slice?
2. **The requirement changes (answer this after finishing Part 1):** imagine that once you'd built your version, the empeo team decided —
    
    > "Before results are opened to employees, every manager has to **calibrate ratings together across teams first** so the standard is aligned — and no employee can see their own result until calibration is locked."
    > 
    
    How well does your design/build absorb this change, and what would you adjust — and how would you know whether the feature actually works (what would you measure)?
    
3. **Working with AI:** was there a point where AI suggested something (on either the design or the code side) and you **disagreed / reworked it / threw it out**? Share one example, and tell us why.
